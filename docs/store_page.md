## Name

Mountain Giant

## Summary (one line, Modrinth "summary" / CurseForge "summary")

A 28-block-tall giant of stone and moss rises out of the mist on some nights and walks the plains until dawn, flattening everything in its way.

## Description (English)

![Mountain Giant](banner)

Some nights the ground starts to shake. Footsteps come from somewhere past the treeline, a thick mist rolls in over the land, and when it slowly lifts, something twenty-eight blocks tall is standing out there.

### The giant

- **There is only one.** A world never has more than one Mountain Giant at a time. It turns up some time between late evening and the small hours on plains, snowy plains or meadows. On any given night there's about a one-in-three chance, every quiet night makes the next one likelier, and by the fourth night it comes for certain.
- **It isn't looking for you.** It's neutral. It walks, it tramples, and it turns back at the edge of its plains or at deep water. Hit it and it will come after you, and its fists hit hard.
- **It flattens things.** Trees, fences, walls and crops in a 28-block-wide path get pushed over. Gentle slopes it walks up; real hills get a pass cut through them. Houses get both fists brought down on them. Rivers and ponds up to six blocks deep it wades straight through.
- **It leaves at dawn**, walking back into the mist it came from.

### Taking it apart

Every 10% of health it loses, a chunk of its body breaks off and drops ore: raw copper first, then raw iron. Bring it down for **10 diamonds**, the **Mountain Heart** and **two Mountain Plates**.

### The Mountain Hammer

Forged from the Mountain Heart. A heavy weapon that also mines like the best pickaxe there is, **3×3 at a time** (sneak to mine one block). Hold right click to raise it, release to **slam the ground**: a shockwave rolls out in three rings and throws everything back.

### Mountain armour

Upgrade netherite armour on a smithing table with a Mountain Plate and a gold ingot. A step above netherite in armour, toughness and knockback resistance, and with the **full set nothing can knock you back**.

### The Mountain Horn

Don't want to wait for the right night? Craft a Mountain Horn from gold blocks, mossy cobblestone and a goat horn, and blow it deep in the night. The ground answers, and the giant rises out of the mist about 80 blocks away. The horn is never used up; after an answer it rests for a day.

### Recipes

**Mountain Hammer** (crafting table)

```
[Iron Block ] [Mountain Heart ] [Iron Block ]
[Mossy Cobble] [Stick          ] [Mossy Cobble]
[           ] [Stick          ] [           ]
```

**Mountain Horn** (crafting table)

```
[Gold Block  ] [Mossy Cobble] [Gold Block  ]
[Mossy Cobble] [Goat Horn   ] [Mossy Cobble]
[Gold Block  ] [Mossy Cobble] [Gold Block  ]
```

**Mountain armour** (smithing table), one piece at a time:

```
Mountain Plate (template slot) + Netherite Helmet / Chestplate / Leggings / Boots + Gold Ingot
```

Two plates drop from every giant, so two giants give you a full set. Enchantments and wear carry over.

### Requirements

## Description (中文)

某些夜晚，大地开始颤动。远处传来沉重的脚步声，大雾漫过平原；等雾慢慢散去，一个二十八格高的石头巨人已经站在那里。

### 巨人

- **世界上只有一只**。它会在深夜出现在平原、雪原或草甸上。每晚大约有三分之一的概率，连续几晚没来就会越来越容易出现，最多第四晚一定会来。
- **它不找你麻烦**。巨人是中立的：它行走、践踏，走到平原边缘或深水前会转身。但如果你打它，它会追过来，拳头非常重。
- **它会踏平一切**。28 格宽的路径上，树木、栅栏、墙和庄稼都会被推倒；缓坡直接走上去，大山会被开出一条路；房屋会被双拳砸碎；6 格深以内的河流和池塘直接蹚过。
- **天亮时离开**，走回它来时的雾里。

### 拆解巨人

它每损失 10% 的生命值，身上就会碎下一块并掉落矿石：先是粗铜，然后是粗铁。击败它可获得 **10 颗钻石**、**山岭之心**和 **2 块山岭石甲片**。

### 山岭巨锤

用山岭之心锻造。既是沉重的武器，也是最强的镐，**一次挖 3×3**（潜行时只挖一格）。按住右键举起，松开**砸向地面**，冲击波分三圈扩散，把周围的一切掀飞。

### 山岭盔甲

在锻造台上，用山岭石甲片和金锭升级下界合金盔甲。护甲值、韧性和击退抗性都比下界合金高一档，**穿齐整套免疫击退**。

### 山岭号角

不想等？用金块、苔石和山羊角合成山岭号角，在深夜吹响。大地会回应，巨人将在约 80 格外从雾中升起。号角不会消耗，召唤成功后需要歇息一天。
